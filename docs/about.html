— Learn Korean, Understand Koreans: Language & Cultural Insights

Who this blog is for

Foreign learners of Korean who want more than dictionary definitions: when Koreans actually use an expression, what nuance translation loses, and how it connects to everyday culture.

How posts are made

Each article follows an editorial outline (hook, untranslatable nuance, real usage, cultural context, reader prompt). Drafting may be assisted by AI tools; examples and structure are checked against a teaching checklist so posts stay useful for learners—not keyword filler.

Categories

  • Untranslatable feelings (e.g. 정, 눈치)
  • Everyday phrases Koreans use often
  • Cultural background behind the language
  • Typical Korean reactions / interjections

Information is for learning purposes. Social nuance varies by age, region, and relationship.
